Big companies in today's news: what they did wrong, who it hurts, and the public filing that shows it.
Fast Shorts on layoffs, lawsuits, fines and settlements, with the document on screen.

Every claim comes from a public record. A 10-K is the yearly report a US public company files with the SEC, the US securities regulator. An 8-K is the report it files when something big happens, like a layoff, a settlement or a new CEO. Court filings and agency complaints (FTC, CFPB, state attorneys general) fill in the rest.

When a regulator sues, we say what it alleges and link the complaint.

Formats: The Receipt (the bill, line by line), Damage Ranking, Who Pays?, Lawsuit Countdown, Before and After.

Sources in the description of every video. Synthetic voice.
