A 9-minute video in 16:9 about one ordinary workday in seven small frictions. Cella, a small blocky black figure with two round white eyes and a crown, stands in colored rooms while on-screen cards give the hour and the moment: 8:05 a.m. good morning, 9:30 the first minute, 11:10 the question, 12:40 the ok, 2:15 the favor, 4:30 the last line and 5:50 the note. Each part shows what the moment costs, and ends with a three-row card with the small fix and a one-line verdict. Four studies appear with an ON THE RECORD stamp: Epley and Schroeder 2014, Flynn and Lake 2008, Huang and colleagues 2017 and Masicampo and Baumeister 2011; the rest carries a CELLA'S OPINION stamp or is plain opinion. In the last part a 45-second clock runs while the viewer is asked to write down which of the seven happens most. It ends on For whoever is carrying one small silence into tomorrow, and Choose friction. The thumbnail shows Cella large with a crown on an amber field, the words One ordinary workday, and a big 7 on a card.
