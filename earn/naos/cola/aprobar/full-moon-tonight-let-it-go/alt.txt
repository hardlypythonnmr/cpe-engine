A vertical film with soft drone music and no voice. Cella, a small figure made of white dots with two dark eyes and no hands, assembles itself out of dust and stands on a horizon of dots under a field of stars. One line of text appears at a time and its key word lights up in moon yellow: Full moon tonight. Write down what weighs on you. Say it out loud, once. Leave the page on the window. Now let it go.
