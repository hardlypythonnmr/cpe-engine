An 11-minute video in 16:9 with two voices, a fast warm host and a calm, dry analyst. In a sunny kitchen, Cella, a small dark doorway-shaped figure with two white eyes, has one email to answer: her coworker Marta asks, Can you send me your part of the report by Friday? A label at the top reads EMAIL: UNSENT. Instead of answering, Cella puts the spice jars in rainbow order, waters the plant and polishes the fridge, while a bar marked MOOD over her head goes down when she opens the email and up with every chore. The analyst explains a 2013 review by Sirois and Pychyl (putting a task off repairs mood for a while) and a 2001 lab study by Tice, Bratslavsky and Baumeister in a gray-blue lab with a candle: a board shows not practicing (of 15), MOOD CAN CHANGE 13.7 min and MOOD "FROZEN" 5.7 min, then ONE STUDY · 2001.
