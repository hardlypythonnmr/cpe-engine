Oṃ Maṇi Padme Hūṃ (sung Om Mani Peme Hung).

What it is: Om Mani Padme Hum, the six-syllable mantra of Avalokiteshvara (Chenrezig), the bodhisattva of compassion, sung here by a woman lead singer and a women's chorus at a kirtan pace. Tibetans say it Om Mani Peme Hung, and that's how it is sung here.

In the tradition: the Dalai Lama explains the jewel, mani, as the compassionate method and the lotus, padme, as wisdom, and the last syllable, Hum, as the two held together.

Sources:
Tenzin Gyatso, the 14th Dalai Lama, On the meaning of Om Mani Padme Hum (sacred-texts.com/bud/tib/omph.htm).
