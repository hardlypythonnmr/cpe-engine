The Home Depot, Apr 7, 2026 (The Home Depot): "Throughout the year, we continued to invest in our associates"
The Home Depot's proxy statement, filed April 7, 2026, reported total compensation of $16,191,127 for its CEO and $37,881 for its median-paid associate for fiscal 2025.

Same proxy: $16.1 million CEO total pay, fiscal 2025. The total compensation of the CEO for fiscal 2025, as reported in the proxy's Summary Compensation Table; mostly stock awards, not cash.
The math: $16.1 million CEO total pay (2025) ÷ $37,881 median pay = about 427 median-worker salaries in a year of CEO pay.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the CEO's total pay divided by the median employee's pay, both from the same proxy. The median employee is a statistical figure the company calculates under the SEC pay ratio rule, not one named person. We round the ratio down; Home Depot's own figure is 427 to 1. Most of the CEO's total is stock awards, not cash. Per the proxy, the median associate is an hourly employee in the U.S. We are not saying anyone is paid too much or too little.
