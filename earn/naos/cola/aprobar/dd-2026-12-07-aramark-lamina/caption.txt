Aramark, Dec 22, 2025 (Aramark, proxy statement): "our commitment to a people-first culture and operational excellence across the industry"
Aramark's proxy statement, filed December 22, 2025, reported total compensation of $14,542,376 for its CEO and $17,573 for its median employee for fiscal 2025.

Same proxy: $14.5 million CEO total pay, fiscal 2025. The total compensation of the CEO for fiscal 2025, as reported in the proxy's Summary Compensation Table; mostly stock and option awards, not cash.
The math: $14.5 million CEO total pay (2025) ÷ $17,573 median pay = about 827 median-worker salaries in a year of CEO pay.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the CEO's total pay divided by the median employee's pay, both from the same proxy. The median employee is a statistical figure the company calculates under the SEC pay ratio rule, not one named person. We round the ratio down; Aramark's own figure is 828 to 1. Most of the CEO's total is stock and option awards, not cash. Per the proxy, the median employee is a full-time U.S. food service worker, identified in fiscal 2024 and kept for fiscal 2025. We are not saying anyone is paid too much or too little.
