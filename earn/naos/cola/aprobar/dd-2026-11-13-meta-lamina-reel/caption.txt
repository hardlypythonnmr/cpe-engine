Meta, Nov 9, 2022 (Mark Zuckerberg, CEO of Meta): "I want to take accountability for these decisions and for how we got here"
Meta's CEO told employees on Nov. 9, 2022 that the company had let more than 11,000 of its employees go. The buyback figure is from Meta's 2022 Form 10-K.

Same year: $27.9 billion in its own stock, 2022, company-wide. $27.96 billion of Class A stock repurchased during 2022, company-wide (Form 10-K).
The math: $27.9 billion in buybacks (2022) ÷ more than 11,000 job cuts = about $2.5 million in buybacks per job cut.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the total spent on buybacks divided by the job count. The job count is "more than 11,000", so the true ratio may be somewhat lower than shown. We are not saying one paid for the other.

Accountability, taken. Shares, bought back. (Our take.)
