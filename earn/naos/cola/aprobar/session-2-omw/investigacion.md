## 2. Real questions
- "Are two a day workouts good?", "working out twice a day good or bad", "working out twice a day vs once", "does working out twice a day help build muscle" (autocomplete).
- Articles that rank (Live Science, GoodRx, Sweat, PureWow): not advised for beginners, risk of overtraining and poor sleep, but splitting one workout into two shorter ones can be less daunting. Reddit results did not surface directly.

## 3. Volume (approximate)
- "two a day workouts" and "training twice a day" both full lists on YouTube (results, muscle gain, fat loss, Dr Mike, Jeff Nippard). "working out twice a day" is the strongest Google phrasing (reddit, results, good or bad). "second workout" is noise (timers, trimester).

## 4. Who ranks
- Fitness YouTubers' evidence videos (Dr Mike, Jeff Nippard) and "I trained twice a day for 30 days" vlogs. Gap: nobody does the funny "you trained ONCE?" family-chat angle.

## 5. Winning term and why
**"training twice a day"** leads the title (matches the video's "you trained once today?" wording and is strong on YouTube). "two-a-days" and "working out twice a day good or bad" are covered in the description with an honest, non-prescriptive answer and a "not a training plan" line.
