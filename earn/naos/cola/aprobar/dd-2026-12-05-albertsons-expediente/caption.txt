Albertsons, Jun 22, 2026 (Albertsons, proxy statement): "our success and ability to delight our customers lies in the engagement of our associates"
Albertsons' proxy statement, filed June 22, 2026, reported total compensation of $16,767,866 for its CEO and $30,978 for its median employee for fiscal 2025.

Same proxy: $16.7 million CEO total pay, fiscal 2025. The total compensation of the CEO for fiscal 2025, as reported in the proxy's Summary Compensation Table.
The math: $16.7 million CEO total pay (2025) ÷ $30,978 median pay = about 541 median-worker salaries in a year of CEO pay.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the CEO's total pay divided by the median employee's pay, both from the same proxy. The median employee is a statistical figure the company calculates under the SEC pay ratio rule, not one named person. We round the ratio down; Albertsons' own figure is 541 to 1. Per the proxy, the median employee is a full-time hourly employee first identified in 2024 and reused, chosen from U.S. employees; small non-U.S. groups were excluded. We are not saying anyone is paid too much or too little.
