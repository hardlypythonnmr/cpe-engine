## 2. Real questions and how people say them
- "How to start Ekadashi fast", "which Ekadashi is best to start fasting", "my first Ekadashi": a real beginner intent exists, phrased as starting.
- "What to eat on Ekadashi / what can I eat during Ekadashi fast", "what to eat after Ekadashi fast", "how to break" (see earlier file).
- Beginner pages (hindutone, thebhaktihouse, mypoojabox, Drik Panchang food page) describe tiers (nirjala, fruit and milk, one grain-free meal) and say beginners need not try nirjala; the day before (Dashami) gets one line at most ("eat simply"). An ISKCON-linked source says that if you break it by mistake, keep going and observe properly; we do not repeat that as a rule, the story just shows Cella ending on time Saturday.
- No useful Reddit thread surfaced on a first Ekadashi; r/fasting talk is about first 20:4 or 16:8 fasts, i.e. the "intermittent fasting week" crowd.

## 4. Who ranks for the long-form terms
- "ekadashi fasting rules" / "how to do ekadashi fast": Sadhguru, ISKCON, Premanand ji Maharaj talks; Hindi/Telugu/Tamil explainers; vlogs in regional languages. English videos are lectures or lists.
- Gap: nothing in English that walks through a whole Ekadashi week (the days before, the eve, the day, pārana, the next date) as a story an outsider enjoys. That is exactly this video. The chapters also index each step for Google "key moments".
