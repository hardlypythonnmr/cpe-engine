## 2. Real questions
- "taking the stairs instead of the elevator benefits", "taking the stairs at work benefits", "taking the stairs every day" (Google).
- MyFitnessPal forum "Taking stairs, any real benefit?": consensus that small extras do little alone but add up. A hospital study cited by several articles: stairs were faster than waiting for the elevator for short trips.
- "elevator for one floor" on Google is about buying home lifts: not our intent.

## 3. Volume (approximate)
- "take the stairs" on YouTube is polluted by songs and memes (Hulk "take the stairs"). "taking the stairs instead of the elevator" is the clean intent phrase on Google. "stairs vs elevator" has its own YouTube demand (parody, race, Mr Bean): funny intent, matches us.

## 4. Who ranks
- Mr Bean stairs vs elevator, race videos, health articles. Gap: a character that chooses the stairs because of a family jab.

## 5. Winning term and why
**"Taking the stairs vs the elevator"** in the title (blends the Google intent phrase with YouTube's funny "stairs vs elevator"). Description answers "is taking the stairs instead of the elevator worth it" honestly, with no calorie numbers.
