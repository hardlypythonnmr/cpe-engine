A 8-minute video in 16:9 about seven things that slide in the week from Christmas to New Year. Cella, a small blocky black figure with two round white eyes and a crown, stands in colored rooms while on-screen cards give each thing: the message, the favor, the borrowed thing, the vague yes, the decision, the list and the apology. Each part says it does not count this week, shows what the person on the other end is waiting for, and ends with a ten-minute fix on a three-row card. One study, Masicampo and Baumeister 2011, appears with an ON THE RECORD stamp; everything else is Cella's opinion. In the last part a 30-second clock runs while Cella waits for the oldest of the seven to be written on a scrap of paper with a date. The thumbnail shows Cella large with a red crown on an amber field, the words This week does not count, and a big 7 on a calendar card.
