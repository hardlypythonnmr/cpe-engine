Skipping the gym again? That's peasant behavior. Cella is sunk into the couch, hears "like a peasant," and takes it personally: up, gym bag, out the door. One squat later she's back on the floor. Then she gets up and goes again.

How do you go to the gym when you don't feel like it? Most people who keep going say the same thing: don't wait to feel motivated. Pack the bag, get out the door, and promise yourself just one exercise. One squat still counts as showing up.
