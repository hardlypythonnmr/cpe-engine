## 3. Volume (approximate)
- Wikipedia "Ekadashi" article: about 6,600 views/month (avg Nov 2025 to Oct 2026). For comparison "Intermittent fasting": about 8,000/month. So Ekadashi is a real, steady English-language interest, with peaks every ~15 days.
- Google autocomplete for "ekadashi" is month-led ("ekadashi in november 2026", "ekadashi december 2026"): people search by date. YouTube is mostly Hindi ("kab hai", "vrat katha"), so English searchers are underserved.

## 4. Who ranks
- YouTube: Hindi/Telugu katha and "kab hai" date videos; Sadhguru/ISKCON talks on rules. Web: panchang date pages. Almost nothing in English that is short, friendly and explains it for an outsider. Gap: a 15-second English explainer with the exact date, the fast forms and parana.

## 5. Winning terms and why
- ekadashi-bet: **"Utpanna Ekadashi 2026"** + "(Dec 4)" in the title (date-led search behavior, exact autocomplete).
- ekadashi-cella-is-fine: **"Ekadashi fast" + "Dec 4 2026"** (so the two pieces don't compete for the identical title query and cover both "ekadashi fasting" and "utpanna ekadashi 2026").
- Both descriptions answer, in the searchers' words: what is Ekadashi, what can you eat on Ekadashi, how do you break the Ekadashi fast (parana), next Ekadashi. Only verified facts used (Drik Panchang, New Delhi; parana 06:59 to 09:04; Mokshada Dec 19 US / Dec 20 Delhi, London). "Not for everyone" and "not instructions" kept. No health claims ("ekadashi fasting benefits" is searched but deliberately not answered).
