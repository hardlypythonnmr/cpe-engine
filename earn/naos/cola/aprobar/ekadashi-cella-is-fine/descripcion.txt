What is Ekadashi? A Hindu fasting day on the 11th day of each half of the lunar month, so it comes about twice a month. Utpanna Ekadashi 2026 falls on Friday, December 4 (Drik Panchang, New Delhi; also December 4 in New York, Los Angeles and London).

What can you eat on Ekadashi? It depends on how you keep it. Some people take only water, some only fruit, some milk, and some eat one meal without grains. Some skip even water. It isn't for everyone.

How do you break the Ekadashi fast? The next morning, in a window called pārana. In New Delhi that's Saturday, December 5, 6:59 to 9:04 am; times change by city, so check a local panchang. Next Ekadashi: Mokshada Ekadashi, Saturday, December 19 in the US (Sunday, December 20 in Delhi and London).
