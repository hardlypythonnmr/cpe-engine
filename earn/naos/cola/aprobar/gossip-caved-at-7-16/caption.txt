Broke Your Ekadashi Fast? Cella Caved at 7:16 pm

Broke your Ekadashi fast? Cella did. Friday, Ekadashi itself. After the cleaning stage, at 7:15 pm, she walks to the fridge, opens it, and a piece of cheese just sticks to her. She has no hands, it's a whole thing. The cheese commits. She eats it. Caved at 7:16, and a neighbor wrote it on the moon calendar. (Cella's times are fiction.)

What is Ekadashi? Ekadashi is a Hindu fast on the 11th day of each half of the lunar month, so it comes about twice a month. This one is Utpanna Ekadashi, on Friday, December 4, 2026 (Drik Panchang, New Delhi; also December 4 in New York, Los Angeles and London).

What can you eat on an Ekadashi fast? It depends on how you keep it. Some people take only water, some only fruit, some milk, and some eat one meal without grains. Some skip even water. It isn't for everyone.

And what if your Ekadashi fast breaks? Traditions answer that differently, so ask your own family, temple or panchang (the Hindu almanac with the lunar dates). In Cella's story, nobody wins and everybody learns something, and the week isn't over yet.
