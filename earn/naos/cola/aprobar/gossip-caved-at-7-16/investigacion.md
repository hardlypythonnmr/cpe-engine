## Real questions and words
Web search "what if ekadashi fast breaks by mistake" (2026-11-19): indiatvnews, Drik Panchang FAQ, an ISKCON Desire Tree forum thread ("what if one by mistake consumes grains on Ekadashi day"), devotional blogs. They agree an accidental break is treated as a lapse, not a disaster, but remedies differ by tradition (prayer, a later vow, fasting the next day). So the copy only says "traditions answer that differently, ask your family, temple or panchang", which is true, and gives no remedy. People's words: "broke", "by mistake", "by accident". "Caved" is our story word; "broke" goes in the title.
