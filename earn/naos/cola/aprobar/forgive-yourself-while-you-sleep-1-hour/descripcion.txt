One hour of self-forgiveness affirmations for sleep, with rain. A soft, slow voice says thirty first-person affirmations for forgiving yourself ("I forgive myself for what I didn't know then", "I did the best I could with what I had", "I let today be over"), each one two or three times with long pauses, so you can repeat them in your head or just let them wash over you. Voice, music and picture made with code by NAOS, no samples.

For the first 10 minutes each affirmation appears on screen while it is said. Then the picture goes dark, the affirmations come back in a new order, a little slower each round, and after 30 minutes the voice slowly gets quieter until nothing is left but the rain. Put it on, turn the brightness down, and let it run.

Background: steady rain, a few closer drops and a low rumble.

Ways to use it: in bed with the screen turned away; on a speaker at low volume (it is mixed quieter than our daytime videos); or on the nights your mind keeps talking to you. It is a video, not a treatment: if sleep or how you feel is hard every night, talk to someone you trust or a doctor.
