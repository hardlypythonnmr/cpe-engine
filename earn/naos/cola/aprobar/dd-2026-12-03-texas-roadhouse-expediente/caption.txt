Texas Roadhouse, Apr 10, 2026 (Texas Roadhouse): "our appreciation for, and commitment to, our employees"
Texas Roadhouse's proxy statement, filed April 10, 2026, reported total compensation of $10,822,133 for its CEO and $20,524 for its median employee for fiscal 2025.

Same proxy: $10.8 million CEO total pay, fiscal 2025. The total compensation of the CEO for fiscal 2025, as reported in the proxy's Summary Compensation Table; mostly stock awards, not cash. The median employee is part-time: about 72% of the company's employees are.
The math: $10.8 million CEO total pay (2025) ÷ $20,524 median pay = about 527 median-worker salaries in a year of CEO pay.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the CEO's total pay divided by the median employee's pay, both from the same proxy. The median employee is a statistical figure the company calculates under the SEC pay ratio rule, not one named person. We round the ratio down; Texas Roadhouse's own figure is 527 to 1. Most of the CEO's total is stock awards, not cash. Per the proxy, about 72% of employees are part-time, and the median employee, a restaurant service assistant, worked about 28 hours a week, so that pay is not full-time pay. We are not saying anyone is paid too much or too little.
