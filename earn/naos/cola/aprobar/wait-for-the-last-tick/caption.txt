Three dots over her head, then two minutes

She stood with three dots over her head while the boxes ticked. When the last one landed she asked for two minutes, and got them.

Waiting for the last box is a pause you can see, and asking for two minutes beats asking for a moment.

For whoever waits for a stopping point and asks for a number of minutes.
