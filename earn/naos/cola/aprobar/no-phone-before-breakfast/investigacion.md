## 2. Real questions
- "How to stop checking phone (first thing) in the morning", "why checking your phone in the morning is bad", "benefits of no phone in the morning", "no phone morning routine".
- r/nosurf and r/getdisciplined threads (mirrors): people describe lying in bed scrolling 30 to 60 minutes; the advice that works is physical (charge it in another room, drawer) and starting with 10 to 30 minutes. One widely shared survey stat (86% check within 10 minutes) is from app marketing, so not used.

## 3. Volume (approximate)
- "no phone before" on both engines is dominated by "no phone before bed". "no phone in the morning" is an exact YouTube suggestion and a Google one with "reddit" and "benefits" variants: smaller but precise. "no phone for a week/30 days/24 hours" is challenge content (bigger, different format).

## 4. Who ranks
- "No phone for 30 days" challenge vlogs and productivity explainers. Gap: a short, funny relapse-then-win story (four minutes, then the drawer).

## 5. Winning term and why
**"No phone in the morning"** leads (exact YouTube suggestion; "before breakfast" has no search demand). Description answers "how to stop checking your phone first thing in the morning" with the drawer trick the video shows.
