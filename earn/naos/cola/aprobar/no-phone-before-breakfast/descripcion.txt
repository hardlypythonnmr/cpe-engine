No phone in the morning, day one: Cella lasted four minutes. The neighbors saw everything. Day two, she puts the phone in the drawer and walks right past it.

How do you stop checking your phone first thing in the morning? Most people who manage it don't rely on willpower. They put the phone somewhere they can't reach from bed, like a drawer or another room, and start small: 10 minutes without it, then 30, then until after breakfast.
