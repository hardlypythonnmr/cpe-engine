Progressive overload, explained by two neighbors at the gym. Same weight every week, and they noticed. Cella knows they see her. She switches to the dumbbell with the +1 kg tag. "One more kilo. Okay."

What is progressive overload? Slowly asking a bit more of your muscles over time: more weight, more reps or more sets. How often should you increase weight? There's no fixed schedule. A common approach is to add reps first and move up in weight once the top of your rep range feels solid. Small jumps count. One kilo counts.
