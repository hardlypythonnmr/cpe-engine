Goodbye from the couch, then out the door

My uncle says goodbye from the couch and walks out without turning. Then I stopped waiting at the door.

People tend to remember how an encounter ended more than how the middle went, so the last few seconds of a visit do a lot of the work.

For whoever turns around at the door to say goodbye.
