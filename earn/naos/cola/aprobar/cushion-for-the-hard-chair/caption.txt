A cushion for the hard chair

The chair is hard and the visit will be long. She slid a cushion onto it, and the guest sat down and sighed.

A cushion takes ten seconds to set out, and the guest sits there for the whole evening.

For whoever puts a cushion on the hard chair before the guest sits.
