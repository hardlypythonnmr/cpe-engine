IBM, Mar 10, 2026 (IBM, proxy statement): "goals focused on employee engagement and talent retention"
IBM's proxy statement, filed March 10, 2026, reported total compensation of $37,989,685 for its CEO and $49,630 for its median employee for 2025.

Same proxy: $37.9 million CEO total pay, 2025. The total compensation of the CEO for 2025, as reported in the proxy's Summary Compensation Table; mostly stock and option awards, not cash.
The math: $37.9 million CEO total pay (2025) ÷ $49,630 median pay = about 765 median-worker salaries in a year of CEO pay.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the CEO's total pay divided by the median employee's pay, both from the same proxy. The median employee is a statistical figure the company calculates under the SEC pay ratio rule, not one named person. We round the ratio down; IBM's own figure is 765 to 1. Most of the CEO's total is stock and option awards, not cash. Per the proxy, the median employee was picked from IBM employees using annual base pay, with foreign currencies converted to U.S. dollars. We are not saying anyone is paid too much or too little.
