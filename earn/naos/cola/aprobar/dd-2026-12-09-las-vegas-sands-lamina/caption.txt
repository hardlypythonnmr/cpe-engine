Las Vegas Sands, Apr 1, 2026 (Las Vegas Sands): "Our Team Members, patrons, suppliers and partners are instrumental"
Las Vegas Sands' proxy statement, filed April 1, 2026, reported total compensation of $31,109,661 for its CEO and $40,215 for its median employee for 2025.

Same proxy: $31.1 million CEO total pay, 2025. The total compensation of the CEO for 2025, as reported in the proxy; it includes the added value of stock awards modified to speed up their vesting.
The math: $31.1 million CEO total pay (2025) ÷ $40,215 median pay = about 773 median-worker salaries in a year of CEO pay.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the CEO's total pay divided by the median employee's pay, both from the same proxy. The median employee is a statistical figure the company calculates under the SEC pay ratio rule, not one named person. We round the ratio down; Las Vegas Sands' own figure is 774 to 1. The CEO's total includes the added value of stock awards modified to speed up their vesting when the CEO moved to a new role; without that change the proxy gives 535 to 1. Per the proxy, the median employee is a full-time employee in Macao, and 98% of the company's employees work outside the U.S. We are not saying anyone is paid too much or too little.
