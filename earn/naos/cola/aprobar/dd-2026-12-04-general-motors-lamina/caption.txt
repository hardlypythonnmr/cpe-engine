General Motors, Apr 20, 2026 (General Motors): "we invest in our employees with market-competitive pay and benefits"
General Motors' proxy statement, filed April 20, 2026, reported total compensation of $29,895,868 for its CEO and $89,785 for its median employee for 2025.

Same proxy: $29.8 million CEO total pay, 2025. The total compensation of the CEO for 2025, as reported in the proxy's Summary Compensation Table; the median employee comes from a global workforce of 155,000 (94,000 in the U.S.).
The math: $29.8 million CEO total pay (2025) ÷ $89,785 median pay = about 332 median-worker salaries in a year of CEO pay.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the CEO's total pay divided by the median employee's pay, both from the same proxy. The median employee is a statistical figure the company calculates under the SEC pay ratio rule, not one named person. We round the ratio down; GM's own figure is 333 to 1. Per the proxy, the median employee comes from a global workforce of 155,000, of whom 94,000 are in the U.S. and 61,000 are not. We are not saying anyone is paid too much or too little.
