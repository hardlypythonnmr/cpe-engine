How to Break Ekadashi Fast: Pārana, Not Spite Pizza

How to break the Ekadashi fast, according to the most adult person in the house. Most people cave, decide they're a failure and eat a whole pizza out of spite. Not Cella. She caved on Friday, and on Saturday morning she still got up and broke the fast properly. On time. With a spoon. Like a lady.

Ekadashi is a Hindu fast on the 11th day of each half of the lunar month, about twice a month. This one, Utpanna Ekadashi, falls on Friday, December 4, 2026 (Drik Panchang, New Delhi; also December 4 in New York, Los Angeles and London).

Do you break the Ekadashi fast the next day? Yes: the next morning, in a window called pārana, the formal breaking of the fast. For this Ekadashi, in New Delhi, pārana is Saturday, December 5, 6:59 to 9:04 am. It changes with where you live, so check your city in a local panchang (the Hindu almanac), like Cella did.
