What Is Ekadashi? Cella Is Doing the Moon This Week

What is Ekadashi? Ask Cella. Monday morning, the whole house is in the kitchen, and she walks in with no breakfast, clears her throat like it's a press conference and says: "This week I'm doing the moon." She has a moon calendar now. It's on the fridge. Facing the fridge. Like a warning.

So, what is Ekadashi? A Hindu fast on the 11th day of each half of the lunar month, so it comes about twice a month, and people have kept it for a very long time. This Friday's is Utpanna Ekadashi: Friday, December 4, 2026 (Drik Panchang, New Delhi; also December 4 in New York, Los Angeles and London). People keep it in different ways: water only, fruit only, milk, or one meal without grains, and some skip even water. It isn't for everyone.

When is the next Ekadashi? Mokshada Ekadashi, Saturday, December 19 in the US (Sunday, December 20 in Delhi and London).
