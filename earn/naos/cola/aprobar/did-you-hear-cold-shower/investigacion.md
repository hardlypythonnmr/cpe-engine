## 2. Real questions
- "How long should a cold shower be (to be effective)?", "how cold should a cold shower be" (YT and Google autocomplete).
- "cold shower benefits male/female/testosterone/huberman" (benefit-seeking; we make no benefit claims).
- r/coldshowers FAQ (mirrors): "I can't stay in a shower for longer than a minute. It's just too much for me!" Answer people give: build up gradually, breathe slowly. Mainstream guides: no universal recommendation, start short; people with heart issues should be careful.

## 3. Volume (approximate)
- "cold shower(s)" and "cold shower benefits" are large head terms. "cold shower challenge" is the first non-benefit suggestion on YouTube ("challenge boy/girl", "30 days") and appears on Google with "rules", "30 day", "wim hof": solid mid volume and matches what the video shows.

## 4. Who ranks
- Long "I took cold showers for 30 days" vlogs (Matt D'Avella and many copies) and benefit explainers (Huberman clips). Shorts are mostly real people screaming under water. Gap: a story about why you'd stay in, with social pressure (the gossip) as the reason.

## 5. Winning term and why
**"cold shower challenge"** in the title and first line (matches the act in the video and the YT autocomplete). Secondary: "how long should a cold shower be" answered in the description without numbers or promises; "hormesis" explained in plain words as allowed ("a small stress your body adapts to"). Safety line kept (not for everyone, heart condition, ask a doctor).
