Cold shower challenge, neighborhood edition. Two neighbors gossip about Cella: she says she's built different. They say she owns a heated blanket. Cella hears every word, steps under the cold water, shivers, and stays.

Why would anyone take a cold shower on purpose? People do it as hormesis: a small stress your body adapts to. The point isn't comfort. It's choosing the harder option for a moment and staying anyway.

How long should a cold shower be? There's no single number. Most people start with a few seconds at the end of a warm shower and build up from there. It isn't for everyone: if you have a heart condition, ask a doctor first.
