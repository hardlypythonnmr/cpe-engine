Verizon, Nov 20, 2025 (Verizon, Form 8-K): "its previously announced plan to reduce its cost structure"
Verizon said in an SEC filing on Nov. 20, 2025 that its workforce reduction would include the elimination of over 13,000 positions. The dividend figure is from Verizon's 2025 Form 10-K.

Same year: $11.48 billion in dividends, 2025, company-wide. $11,481 million of dividends paid in 2025, company-wide (cash flow statement, Form 10-K, in millions; columns are 2025, 2024, 2023); Verizon reports no buybacks in this line.
The math: $11.48 billion in dividends (2025) ÷ more than 13,000 job cuts = about $883,153 in dividends per announced job cut.
This is a ratio, not a payment and not a cause: the dividends paid divided by the job count. The $11.481 billion is the dividends Verizon paid in 2025, company-wide; it is not buybacks. The filing says "over 13,000" positions, so the real figure per cut is a little lower than the result. We are not saying one paid for the other.
