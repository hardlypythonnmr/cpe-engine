Loy Krathong is a Thai festival held on the full moon of the 12th lunar month. People set a small boat of folded banana leaf, with a candle and flowers, on the water and make a wish. In Thai, loy means float and krathong means a small vessel. Happy Loy Krathong to everyone at the water this full moon. Corrections welcome.
