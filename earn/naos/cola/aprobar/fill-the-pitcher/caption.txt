The empty pitcher goes to the tap first

He pours the last glass and the pitcher is empty. It goes back to the tap before it goes back to the shelf.

Refilling takes thirty seconds now, and an empty pitcher on the shelf is a small chore waiting for whoever is thirsty next.

For whoever puts the pitcher back full, not empty.
