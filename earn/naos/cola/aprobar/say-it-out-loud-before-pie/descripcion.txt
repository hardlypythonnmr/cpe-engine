The study: in three experiments, people wrote gratitude letters and then guessed how the receivers would feel. They underestimated how positive receivers would feel and overestimated how awkward it would be. The limit: the experiments were online and tested written letters, not a thank-you said out loud at a table. Cella's scene is a story, not a result.
Kumar, A., & Epley, N. (2018). Undervaluing gratitude: Expressers misunderstand the consequences of showing appreciation. Psychological Science, 29(9), 1423-1435. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797618772506
